VisiMark

a spreadsheet mechanics for Markdown

and it catches your AI arithmetic hallucinations, too!

Author

Write Net = Price * Qty instead of 50.00. The document computes itself and still renders on GitHub, in previews, through pandoc — no plugin.

| Item | Price | Qty |   Net |
|------|------:|----:|------:|
| pen  |  5.00 |  10 | 50.00 |

```vmark #order
Net   = Price * Qty
total = SUM(Net)
```

Total: **50.00**<!--vmark=order.total-->

Adopt

Already have a table full of numbers? visimark infer derives the rules that reproduce them, so you don't retype anything.

$ visimark infer figures.md

  column rules
    Net = Price * Qty    4/4 ok
    VAT = Net * 0.23     4/4 ok

  2 rules, 3 scalars written.

Enforce

visimark check fails CI the moment a number and its formula disagree. This is the one that matters — Author and Adopt exist to get you here.

$ visimark check figures.md
✓ figures.md — 14 values agree
$ echo $?
0
